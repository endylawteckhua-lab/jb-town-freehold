常见问题 · FAQ

关于地点、资格、贷款的常见问题

Location, eligibility & financing — answers to what buyers ask most.

这里离 CIQ 关卡和 RTS Link 有多远？How far is this from JB CIQ Checkpoint and the RTS Link?

驾车大约 10 分钟就能到 JB CIQ 关卡——也就是连接新加坡 Woodlands 的通关口。RTS Link 的 Bukit Chagar 车站就设在 CIQ 关卡旁边，车程时间相近。未来 RTS 通车后，从这里到新加坡的衔接会更加方便。

It's about a 10-minute drive to the JB CIQ Checkpoint — the crossing point to Singapore's Woodlands. The RTS Link's Bukit Chagar station sits right next to the CIQ complex, so travel time is similar. Once the RTS Link opens, getting to Singapore will be even more convenient.

我拿了新加坡 PR，会被当成外国人买家吗？I hold Singapore PR status — will I be treated as a foreign buyer?

不会。只要你还持有马来西亚国籍（就算你已经是新加坡 PR），买这里跟本地人一样——没有 RM500,000 最低价限制，也不需要缴付外国人 Levy Fee 或额外的 MOT 印花税，Type A 单位从 RM300,000 起就能入手。（如果你已经放弃马来西亚国籍、归化新加坡公民，则适用外国人购屋规则，详情请 WhatsApp 询问。）

No. As long as you still hold Malaysian citizenship (even as a Singapore PR), you buy here just like a local — no RM500,000 minimum price, and no foreigner Levy Fee or extra MOT stamp duty. Type A units start from RM300,000. (If you've renounced Malaysian citizenship for Singapore citizenship, foreign-buyer rules apply — WhatsApp us for details.)

新币收入可以用来申请房贷吗？Can I use my SGD income to apply for a home loan?

可以。不少在新加坡工作的买家，都是用新币收入向马来西亚银行申请贷款，银行会按汇率把新币收入换算成马币来评估你的还款能力。能贷多少、需要准备什么文件，每个人情况不同，直接 WhatsApp 我们，帮你对接银行做初步评估。

Yes. Many buyers working in Singapore use their SGD income to apply for a loan with Malaysian banks, with SGD income converted to MYR to assess loan eligibility. The exact amount and required documents vary by person — WhatsApp us and we'll help connect you with a bank for an initial assessment.

CPF 可以用来买这里的房子吗？Can I use my CPF savings to buy this property?

老实说：根据 CPF 局的规定，CPF 公积金一般只能用于新加坡国内的房产，不能直接用来购买包括新山在内的海外房产。所以如果计划用 CPF 来付这里的房子，目前是不可行的。

不过这正好说明这个项目的优势——你完全不需要动用 CPF。单靠你的新币现金储蓄或月薪，换算成马币后，在新山这里的购买力远超你的想象。每月 SGD 430 起，就能成为这里的屋主。

To be upfront: under CPF rules, CPF savings can generally only be used for property within Singapore — not for overseas property including JB. So using CPF directly for this isn't an option right now.

But that's exactly what makes this stand out — you don't need your CPF at all. Your SGD cash savings or monthly income alone, converted to MYR, go a lot further here than you'd expect. From SGD 430/month, you could own a home here.

这是新盘吗？现在买有什么价格优势？Is this a new launch? What's the price advantage of buying now?

是的，这是全新预售项目（New Project / New Launch），目前是第一批 + Early Bird 价格，是整个项目里最低的价格阶段，第二、三、四批会逐步涨价。如果你是用"新币收入投资新山新盘"的角度来看，越早入场，成本越低，未来出租或转手的空间也越大。

Yes, this is a brand-new presale project (New Project / New Launch), currently at First Batch + Early Bird pricing — the lowest tier across the whole project, with prices rising in batches 2–4. If you're viewing this as an SGD-income investment into a new JB project, the earlier you commit, the lower your cost — and the more upside for rental yield or resale later.
